## Setup into the open
US markets are open today; the session is the last full one before Thanksgiving. Futures are lower after Monday's outsized rally: the Nasdaq Composite gained 6.51%, the S&P 500 4.73% and the Dow 3.01%, the strongest session since April 2025. The trigger was Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO about two weeks after he resigned, which lifted Nvidia more than 30% ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/us-stock-futures-mixed-nvidia-101442654.html)). Premarket, Nasdaq-100 futures were down about 1.7%, S&P futures about 0.8–0.9% and Dow futures about 0.4% ([TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262319957-us-pre-market-trump-jensen-huang-nvidia-ai-chips-xau-wti-oil-gold-ant-tradingkey)).

**Sentiment:** VIX near 28.5 (up about 7% premarket); 10-year yield about 4.92%; WTI about $80, Brent about $86–88; gold about $3,620; Bitcoin about $59.5k, down about 2.8%. A dollar reading wasn't available in the sources I could read.

Monday's big movers (Dell +20%, Micron +19%, Oracle +12%) are candidates for reversal or follow-through. Nvidia's Huang is expected to face congressional questions on China sales and export compliance.
