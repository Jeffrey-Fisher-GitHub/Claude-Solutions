## Overnight recap
Thursday's AI/chip rout was severe: the S&P 500 fell 4.4% to 7,353.73 (worst day since April 2025), the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.5% to 24,315 (correction territory), the Dow fell 3.4%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped about 12%. Europe was calmer: Stoxx 600, DAX, FTSE about -0.4%, CAC +0.3%; ASML -6.6%. Reported drivers: fears of tighter AI regulation and slower data-center buildout after the Democratic midterm sweep, plus reports of Microsoft cooling on neocloud projects.
