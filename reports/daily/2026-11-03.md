## Teaching ideas for CISC 121

1. **Lab: "Why did the Nasdaq move?" data exercise.** Students pull a week of index and AI-stock prices (CSV), compute daily returns, and fit a simple regression or compare moves around earnings dates (Amazon, Alphabet, Oracle). Discuss why prediction is hard, overfitting, and why this is informational, not advice.
2. **Lecture + demo: multi-agent orchestration and its failure modes.** Use DeepSeek's decompose-and-delegate agent and the arXiv papers on long-horizon tool-call state tracking and agent-fleet safety. Students sketch an orchestrator/worker design, then identify where it can drift, lose state, or need a human checkpoint.
3. **Lab: Java meets ML hygiene.** Using JDK 27 (or 21 LTS), students write a small k-nearest-neighbors classifier, write tests (JUnit, optionally with Spring-style fluent assertions), and benchmark it with and without bulk-copy and collection choices. Close with a short debate on "clean code vs pragmatism" using the two opinion pieces.
