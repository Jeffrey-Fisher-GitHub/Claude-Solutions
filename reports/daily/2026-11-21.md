## (d) Academic articles

**Language Models Make New Research Sound Old** - [arXiv 2611.23546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23546)
Finds LLMs tend to describe open questions as partly studied, even though they often judge correctly when checking papers separately. A fixed-answer correction raised accurate reporting sharply.

**Large Language Models Have Not Yet Transformed Science** - [arXiv 2611.22975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22975)
Analyzing 2.5 million full-text papers across five disciplines, the authors find most pre-2023 trends continued with few breaks after LLMs appeared. Some exceptions appear, such as faster growth of Asian computer-science coauthor teams.

**Worldviews in Large Language Models** - [arXiv 2611.22670](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22670)
Argues models carry measurable worldviews, often bleaker than human ones, that seem to come from post-training. System prompts and model-to-model dialogue can shift them.

**Large Language Models without QK-Normalization** - [arXiv 2611.10560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10560)
Proposes QK-MAP, a magnitude-aware replacement for QK-Norm in attention, reporting lower loss from 190M to 7B parameters.

**Large Language Models for AI Infrastructure (survey)** - [arXiv 2611.19061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19061)
Organizes work on using LLMs to design and operate training and serving infrastructure, noting that reported gains depend on whether evaluation used simulators or production systems.

## (e) Java and better code

**Java4Shell, CVE-2026-114938** - [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/critical-java4shell-flaw-allows-rce-attacks-on-major-java-applications/)
A memory-corruption bug in OpenJDK's hardware-accelerated Base64 decoder (x86-64 with AVX-512 VBMI) can leak memory or enable remote code execution; researchers reported exploits against Jenkins, Jira, Confluence and Keycloak. Workarounds exist (disable the intrinsic or limit AVX), but patching is the real fix.

**Microsoft Build of OpenJDK November CPU** - [Microsoft Java blog](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/java/java-openjdk-november-2026-critical-patch-update/)
Patched builds 25.0.5.1, 21.0.13.1, 17.0.21.1 and 11.0.33.1 shipped, with the CVE fix in the 25, 21 and 17 lines. Microsoft plans to end JDK 11 and 17 support in September 2027.

**Oracle JDK 21 licence change** - [Java Bulletin digest](https://javabulletin.substack.com/p/digest-111-the-jdk-21-licence-change)
From the October 2026 update, Oracle JDK 21 moves from no-fee terms to the Java SE OTN licence; personal, development and testing use stay free. Oracle points users to JDK 25.

**JEP 547 Lazy Constants proposed for JDK 28** - [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/java-news-roundup-nov09-2026/)
After three preview rounds with no changes, the feature was proposed to target JDK 28, with early-access builds already out. Secondhand details put JDK 28 GA in March 2027.

**Spring Boot 4.2.0-RC2 and Spring AI 2.1.0-RC2** - [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/java-news-roundup-nov09-2026/)
Boot adds WebFlux forwarded-prefix handling and a Redis fix; Spring AI adds a DecisionModel API and a vector-store similarity method. Boot GA is expected this month.

**G1 default GC on all JDK 27 machines** - [Java Bulletin digest](https://javabulletin.substack.com/p/digest-111-the-jdk-21-licence-change)
JDK 27 no longer falls back to Serial GC on small machines, which can raise native memory use and trigger out-of-memory kills in small containers.

## Teaching ideas for CISC 121

1. **Lab: Is the AI trade a bubble? Data and uncertainty.** Students pull historical price data for Nvidia and a semiconductor ETF (CSV provided), compute daily returns and volatility, and flag outlier days. They then discuss why headline numbers differed across sources (Nasdaq -3% vs -5%, intraday vs close) and practice source verification. Ties to model-risk and AI-economics themes.
2. **Lecture and activity: Agent safety case studies.** Use the Meta Muse data-mixing report, the California v. OpenAI sandbox-escape complaint and the Gemini Mac computer-use launch to walk through permissions, least privilege, human approval gates and logging. Groups design a safety checklist for an agent that controls a desktop, mapped to the NIST AI RMF in the course materials.
3. **Lab: Does the LLM overclaim? Replicate a mini version of the "research sounds old" finding.** Students ask an LLM whether a handful of topics are studied, check against arXiv search results, and tally overstatement. Pair it with a Java exercise: a small program that reads a CSV of results and computes accuracy, using JUnit tests, and a short discussion of the Java4Shell patch lesson (keeping runtimes and dependencies current).
