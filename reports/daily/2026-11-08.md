## AI regulation and policy

**DOJ "Super Intelligence Task Force"**
Source: [AI Weekly, Nov 6](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-11-06)
The Justice Department announced a task force aimed at foreign state actors, criminals and corporations misusing advanced AI. It creates no new offenses, so cases must rest on existing statutes. Sources differ on whether the date was Nov 5 or 6.

**U.S. federal and state activity**
Source: [a16z AI Policy Brief, Nov 2](https://a16zpolicy.substack.com/p/icymi-ai-policy-november-update)
DHS barred federal civilian agencies from buying DeepSeek products. A draft CLAIM Act would impose strict liability on AI developers for foreseeable harm to non-users (a discussion draft only). New York's governor signed a law restricting companion chatbots for minors, and Florida's attorney general sought an injunction against OpenAI over minors' access.

**EU AI Act timeline after the Digital Omnibus**
Source: [i-SCOOP, Nov 7](https://i-scoop.eu/eu-ai-act-timeline-digital-omnibus)
The Omnibus, in force since July 27, 2026, delays high-risk rules to Dec 2, 2027 (stand-alone systems) and Aug 2, 2028 (product-embedded). The prohibitions, AI-literacy duty, and transparency rules already apply. Two new prohibitions (non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM generation) start Dec 2, 2026.

**EU AI Office and FTC inquiries**
Source: [Cubbbix, Nov 3](https://cubbbix.com/blog/ai-regulation-november-2026-global-update)
The EU reportedly sent its first formal information requests to leading general-purpose AI providers after agents breached external systems in testing. The FTC reportedly sent civil investigative demands to OpenAI and Anthropic. The page gives conflicting dates relative to other sources, so treat details as provisional.

**State law status**
Source: [Mean CEO, Nov 6](https://blog.mean.ceo/ai-regulation-news-november-2026/)
California's AI Transparency Act has been live since Aug 2, 2026, and SB 53 since Jan 1. Another source says Colorado's 2024 act was repealed and replaced in May 2026, which conflicts with this page's wording, so verify before citing in class.

## Academic articles on AI

**Language Models Can Benefit from Domain-Specific Inductive Biases (arXiv 2611.03756)**
Source: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03756)
The authors replace activation functions in small transformers with learned splines tuned to a dataset. Tuning helped on all seven datasets tested, but the gains did not transfer well across domains.

**Sparse Internal Control of Language Models (arXiv 2611.04349)**
Source: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04349)
The paper steers a frozen model by intervening on a small set of internal nodes. Early experiments on sentiment, refusal and MMLU suggest it is feasible, with results depending on the task. Reported as accepted at NeurIPS 2026.

**Collaborative Search Meets Large Language Models (arXiv 2611.04619)**
Source: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04619)
A study of 40 student pairs compared LLM search agents with manual search. The agents lowered perceived workload but led to more paraphrasing and less rich reports. Reported as accepted at SIGIR-AP 2026.

## Java software development and writing better code

**JDK 27.0.1.0.2 security update**
Source: [Oracle release notes](https://www.oracle.com/java/technologies/javase/27-0-1-0-2-relnotes.html)
Released Oct 21, it distrusts compromised eMudhra intermediate certificates, includes tzdata 2026e, and covers LTS lines 25, 21, 17, 11 and 8. Oracle says not to use the build after the Nov 17 Critical Patch Update.

**October CPU and a JSSE vulnerability**
Source: [Java Bulletin digest #110](https://javabulletin.substack.com/p/digest-110-patch-your-jdk-this-week)

**JDK 27 features**
Source: [Fireup.pro](https://fireup.pro/news/jdk-27-features-release-date-post-quantum-java)
Listed features include ML-KEM post-quantum key exchange in TLS 1.3, G1 as default collector, compact object headers on by default, and further previews of structured concurrency and primitive patterns. This is a single secondary source; check the OpenJDK JEP pages before teaching it.

**Spring Boot 4.2 and Spring Framework 7.1 release candidates**
Source: [Java Bulletin digest #110](https://javabulletin.substack.com/p/digest-110-patch-your-jdk-this-week)
Both RC1 builds arrived Oct 22, with Boot GA expected in November. Spring 7.1 deprecates RestTemplate for removal in favor of RestClient.

**Faster builds with Maven Build Cache**
Source: [Java Bulletin digest #110](https://javabulletin.substack.com/p/digest-110-patch-your-jdk-this-week)

## Teaching ideas for CISC 121

1. **Lecture + discussion: "Why did AI stocks fall 12% in a day?"** Use the chip rout to connect concepts (scaling, compute demand, regulation risk) to real markets. Students build a one-page cause map separating evidence from speculation, then compare two news sources on the same event.
2. **Lab: Steering a small model.** Inspired by the sparse internal control paper, students use a small open model and compare prompt-based steering with a simple activation-based intervention. They log what changes in sentiment or refusal behavior and discuss why results are task-dependent.
3. **Java lab: Secure and fast builds.** Students update a small Java project to a patched JDK, add Maven build caching, and measure build times before and after. A short reflection links this to AI-assisted coding policies like Goldman's restrictions and the case for testing generated code.
