## 2. AI regulation and policy

Source: https://www.nga.org/news/press-releases/governors-call-for-ai-action/ (summarized via a secondary blog, https://explainx.ai/blog/nga-governors-federal-frontier-ai-state-consumer-rules-november-2026; NGA original not fetched)
On Nov 22 the NGA released a statement of principles, not a law. ## 4. Academic articles

**A statistical measure of trust in generative AI** (arXiv 2611.30286)
Source: https://arxivsignals.io/papers/2611.30286
The authors score each generated token by how typical it is under an expert model's distribution, then combine 15 response-level indicators to flag likely errors and hallucinations. Detectors transferred across program generation, math and medical QA, though not symmetrically.

**Adopting AI Technologies without Traps** (arXiv 2611.30200)
Source: https://arxivsignals.io/papers/2611.30200
Models long-term AI adoption as a multi-agent reinforcement learning problem where harms such as skill erosion appear as negative transitions or rewards. Results depend on the assumed erosion model.

**Pruning DNA Sequence** (arXiv 2611.10386) and **LAAM energy-efficient retrieval** (arXiv 2611.16488)
Source: https://arxivtldr.org/weekly
Two efficiency-focused picks: one skips easily predicted genomic tokens during pretraining to cut data processing sharply, the other approximates LLM relevance judgments to save retrieval energy on long queries. Percentages are the site's summaries; check the papers.

**Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots** (arXiv 2611.18562)
Source: https://arxivtldr.org/weekly
Reinforcement learning in simulation transferred to real dual-arm robots, a nice sim-to-real example.

## 5. Java and better code

**Spring Boot 4.2.0 released**
Source: https://spring.io/blog/2026/11/19/spring-boot-4-2-0-available-now/
GA on Nov 19. Adds AMQP 1.0 support, opt-in OpenTelemetry semantic conventions, SSL bundles for LDAP, registry-backed buildpack caches and faster executable-jar startup. RestTemplate support is deprecated in favor of RestClient, and Pulsar support is deprecated.

**Oracle changes JDK 21 update licensing**
Source: https://blogs.oracle.com/java/jdk-21-approaches-end-of-permissive-license (via https://javabulletin.substack.com/p/digest-111-the-jdk-21-licence-change; Oracle post not fetched)
From the October 2026 CPU, JDK 21 updates ship under the Java SE OTN licence rather than NFTC. Personal use, development, testing and demos stay free; Oracle recommends JDK 25, which stays under NFTC until October 2028. Teaching labs should consider OpenJDK distributions or JDK 25.

**JDK roadmap**
Source: https://javabulletin.substack.com/p/digest-111-the-jdk-21-licence-change
Per the newsletter, JDK 27 reached GA Sept 15, and JDK 28 enters rampdown phase one Dec 3 with GA targeted for March 9, 2027.

## Teaching ideas for CISC 121

1. **Hallucination-detection lab.** Using the token-typicality idea from arXiv 2611.30286, have students compute per-token log-probabilities from a small open model and flag low-probability tokens in answers, then discuss precision/recall of such a detector.
2. **Policy debate and risk-framework mapping.** Students compare the NGA's federal/state split with the NIST AI RMF (already in the course project), assigning a sample AI product (e.g., a medical model like LingSante) to federal vs. state oversight and justifying it.
3. **Java migration mini-lab.** Have students move a small ML-inference service from a RestTemplate-based client to RestClient on Spring Boot 4.2, and write a short note on JDK licensing choices (JDK 21 vs. 25 distributions). Add a stretch task: a market-data time-series anomaly detector in Java to examine the Nov 23 move as an outlier.
