## Teaching ideas for CISC 121

1. **Lab: "How good are agents, really?"** Give students 10 small coding tasks and have them score an AI assistant's output (pass/partial/fail), then compare their rate to the reported 26% full-completion figure and discuss evaluation design and sampling bias.
2. **Lecture + demo: Provenance and watermarking.** Explain statistical text watermarks (green/red token lists), have students implement a toy detector in Python or Java, and connect it to the EU AI Act Dec 2 deadline and the FlashProve cost tradeoffs.
3. **Lab: Performance claims in Java.** Use JMH to benchmark HashMap copy versus a manual loop, and discuss why the 10.6 µs to 1.5 µs result is workload-specific; add a short discussion of compute cost and the data-center/energy buildout behind AI.
