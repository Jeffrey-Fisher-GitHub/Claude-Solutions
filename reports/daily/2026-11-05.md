## 1. AI industry news

**OpenAI agents hunt for Linux root-access flaws** - [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/a-thousand-fewer-paths-to-root/)
An automated security pipeline flagged 2,308 possible flaws in the Linux kernel, reproduced 1,724, and produced working exploits for 948 local privilege-escalation bugs. The run cost roughly $1.2M in API credits, and maintainers had already patched dozens upstream.

**Claude assists with vault particle structure** - [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/investigating-vault-particles-with-claude)
Researchers used Claude to help determine the first 3D structure of a prokaryotic vault particle, a cell structure found in 1986 whose function is still unknown. Science magazine's coverage says the work deepens the mystery.

**Meta acquires AI coding startup Conductor** - [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/meta-acquires-ai-coding-startup-conductor/)

## 2. AI regulation and policy

**EU AI Act: two Dec 2, 2026 deadlines** - [Cubbbix summary](https://cubbbix.com/blog/ai-regulation-november-2026-global-update)
New prohibitions on AI that generates non-consensual intimate imagery or CSAM take effect, and the grace period for machine-readable marking of generated content ends. The Digital Omnibus pushed high-risk obligations out to 2027-2028. (Secondary source.)

**DHS bars DeepSeek from federal civilian agencies** - [a16z policy brief](https://a16zpolicy.substack.com/p/icymi-ai-policy-november-update); [Federal Register notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/20/2026-21447/notice-of-order-regarding-exclusion-and-removal-of-deepseek-products-from-federal-civilian-executive)
An Oct 20 order requires agencies to stop buying DeepSeek products and remove existing ones, including third-party tools built on its models.

**New York companion-chatbot law for minors** - [Cubbbix summary](https://cubbbix.com/blog/ai-regulation-november-2026-global-update); [bill S9051](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S9051)
S 9051B reportedly restricts AI companions from claiming to be human, flattering users, or promoting self-harm for minors, effective Jan 1, 2027. Signing date is reported only by one secondary source.

**Connecticut SB 5 provisions in force** - [a16z policy brief](https://a16zpolicy.substack.com/p/icymi-ai-policy-november-update)
Since Oct 1, parts of the state's AI act apply, including whistleblower protections at frontier developers and limits on discriminatory automated tools for workers.

**China tightens cybersecurity inspections** - [a16z policy brief](https://a16zpolicy.substack.com/p/icymi-ai-policy-november-update)
New police-led inspection measures took effect Oct 1 and let authorities check companies' algorithm-security duties and run remote vulnerability tests.

## Teaching ideas for CISC 121

1. **Agents and cost per outcome (lab):** Using OpenAI's kernel-exploit numbers ($1.2M for 948 exploits), have students compute cost per success, precision (1,724 of 2,308 reproduced) and discuss dual-use risk and responsible disclosure.
2. **Open vs. closed weights debate (lecture + activity):** Compare Mistral Large 4's open-weight license with gated Gemini 4 Carbon. Students tabulate license terms, pricing per million tokens, and discuss why self-reported benchmarks need independent verification.
3. **Java for AI (lab):** Build a small LangChain4j app calling a local Ollama model, then add a simple confidence-threshold fallback (inspired by 1.23's cascade planner) and unit tests, tying in clean-code and testing practice.
