## Teaching ideas for CISC 121

1. **Agent guardrails lab.** Using the agent-security and governance news, students build a small tool-using agent in Python with an allow-list of actions and a logging layer, then red-team each other's agents for prompt-injection and over-permission failures.
2. **AutoML in miniature.** After discussing the LLM-as-searcher paper, students run a manual versus random versus greedy hyperparameter search on a small dataset and compare results, then discuss why search strategy and exploration versus exploitation matter.
3. **Market-shock data exercise.** Give students a small price and volume dataset for AI-related stocks around the Nov 5 and Nov 13 selloffs; they compute returns and volatility, build a simple anomaly detector, and debate what ML can and cannot say about "news-driven" moves. Pair with a short discussion on the Dec 2 EU marking deadline and why provenance matters for generated content.
