# Castaway Island — the plan

Twelve people, one 1,200-acre island, two years. This is the design the simulation (`src/sim.py`) follows, and every number below is what the simulation actually produced (seed 1200).

---

## 1. The island

| | |
|---|---|
| **Area** | 1,200 acres (4.9 km²), roughly 3.0 km east–west by 2.1 km north–south |
| **Location** | Remote South Pacific, ~15° S. Wet season Nov–Apr, dry season May–Oct |
| **Coastline** | About 9 km. A sheltered lagoon on the south side behind a fringing reef; two good fishing reefs; one sea cave |
| **Mountain** | A 412 m volcanic peak, north of centre. Bare rock summit, rainforest on the slopes. From the top you can see the whole island and nothing else |
| **Fresh water** | A permanent river falls off the mountain (a waterfall about halfway down) and runs south into the lagoon right beside camp. A second spring on the north side keeps flowing in the dry season |
| **Forest** | Mixed tropical hardwood and bamboo on the slopes (~120,000 trees), coconut palms along every beach |
| **Food** | Reef fish, coconuts, breadfruit, wild bananas, papaya, wild taro and yams. Later: six garden terraces |
| **Other** | Clay in the riverbank (pottery, kiln), a dry cave above the river (storm shelter), salt from the lagoon flats |

**Why the south beach for camp:** it's inside the reef (calm water, safe canoe launches), next to the river mouth (water without hauling), close to the forest edge (timber, shade), and faces the open sea where ships pass.

## 3. Priorities (the order everything is built in)

1. **Get found** — fire on the beach from night 1, a beacon on the summit, a giant SOS.
2. **Water** — the river, then a bamboo aqueduct so nobody hauls water.
3. **Shelter** — lean-to in week 1, then real huts off the ground, then a storm-proof hall.
4. **Food** — fishing and coconuts first, then preservation so a storm doesn't mean hunger, then gardens.
5. **Health** — clean water, a latrine away from the river, an infirmary, rest days.
6. **Sustainability** — every tree cut is replanted; gardens instead of stripping the wild groves.

## 4. Phased plan and what actually happened

### Phase 1 — Survive (days 1–14)
| Day | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1 | Beach signal fire lit. It burns every night for two years |
| 2 | Wreck salvaged: galley stores, life rafts, crash axe, first-aid kits. River found |
| 7 | Palm-frond lean-to finished (an orange life raft becomes part of the roof) |
| 12 | Fishing lines, spears and a net made — fish catch roughly doubles |
| 14 | Sam and Lucy reach the summit: no other land in sight |

People ate about **89%** of what they needed in month one and average health fell to the mid-70s.

## 5. The numbers

**Daily need:** 2,300 kcal per working adult, 1,900 when sick or resting — about **27,600 kcal/day** for the group.

| Source | How it's gathered | Two-year total |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | Lines and spears → weir → dugout → outrigger | **10,277 kg** |
| Coconuts | Beach and palm groves (capped at ~2 per person per day) | **20,318** |
| Fruit | Breadfruit, bananas, papaya, mango (seasonal) | **8,208 kg** |
| Wild roots | Taro and yams | **2,601 kg** |
| Gardens | Six terraces, first harvest ~110 days after planting | **5,751 kg** |

| Period | Avg fish/day | Food eaten vs need | Avg health | Avg morale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | 4.9 kg | 89% | 75 | 60 |
| Days 31–90 | 9.8 kg | 95% | 74 | 65 |
| Days 91–180 | 11.5 kg | 96% | 88 | 79 |
| Days 181–365 | 14.2 kg | 100% | 98 | 86 |
| Days 366–730 | 16.1 kg | 100% | 99 | 84 |

**Timber:** 171 trees felled for 32 building projects (about 3 logs per tree); **627 seedlings planted** from the nursery — a net gain for the forest.

**Weather over two years:** 314 clear days, 174 cloudy, 217 rain, 23 squalls, 2 cyclones.

**Health:** 135 person-days lost to illness or injury, all twelve recovered. Nobody died.

## 6. A normal day (after month six)

| Job | People | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fishing | 2–4 | One crew in the canoe/outrigger, one checking the weir |
| Foraging | 1–2 | Coconuts, breadfruit, bananas |
| Gardening | 1–2 | Walt and June, weeding and harvesting |
| Building / repairs | 3–5 | The current project |
| Felling trees | 0–3 | Only when a project needs logs |
| Cooking and preserving | 1 | Elena; smoking any surplus fish |
| Fire watch | 1 | Beach fire by day, summit beacon at night |
| Nursing | 0–1 | Only when someone is sick or hurt |

Sundays: no building. Fishing, food and fires only; everyone else rests.

## 7. The video

The **last 15 seconds of every day** — the golden hour sinking into night at the beach camp — for all **730 days** (3 hours 2 minutes in total). Each clip is driven by that day's simulation state:

- **Sky and light:** real sunset time and moon phase for the date, the weather (clear, cloudy, rain, squall, cyclone), stars, lightning
- **The camp:** every structure drawn at its actual build progress and storm damage; gardens grow; trees disappear as they're felled and saplings appear as they're replanted; the wreck on the reef until the first cyclone moves it
- **The people:** all twelve walk back from wherever they worked that day (shore, forest, garden, building site) to the fire; the sick lie under the lean-to
- **HUD:** day counter, date, season, weather and moon; food store, catch, timber, projects built and in progress; who did what; the island map with fog-of-war that clears as they explore; the day's headline; a roster with each person's health
